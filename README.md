# 🚛 FreightShorts

[![Launch FreightShorts App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Launch_Live_App-FreightShorts_6.3-38bdf8?style=for-the-badge&logo=apple&logoColor=090d16)](https://deepakkr.github.io/FreightShorts/)

**FreightShorts** is a mobile-first news aggregation PWA app for the trucking, middle-mile, and supply chain industry. It aggregates live industry news into a fast, Inshorts-style vertical card deck, weaves in a 3-tier Domain Academy, includes a hands-free (scrappy) Podcast mode, and uses **Google Gemini AI** for live quantitative synthesis and interactive Q&A.

🔗 **Live App URL:** **[https://deepakkr.github.io/FreightShorts/](https://deepakkr.github.io/FreightShorts/)**

##  Step 2: Enable Free Google Gemini AI Features (60 Seconds)

The live news feed, 3-tier Domain Academy, and Podcast audio work immediately out of the box with zero setup. To unlock real-time **AI Synthesis (`✨ AI`)** and **Ask Gemini Q&A**, add a free Google Gemini API key:

1. Open **[Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey)](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey)** in your browser and sign in with any personal `@gmail.com` account.
2. Tap **Create API key** -> **Create API key in new project**.
3. Tap the **Copy** icon next to your newly generated key string (supports both `AQ...` and `AIzaSy...` key formats).
4. Open the **FreightShorts** app from your phone's Home Screen and tap **`⚙️ Key`** in the top-right corner.
5. Paste your key into the box and tap **`Test & Auto-Discover Model`**.
   - *The app will automatically detect the newest active Gemini model on your account and display `✅ Connected`.*
6. Tap **`Save & Close`**. The top-right button will turn green (`⚙️ Key ✓`).

> [!NOTE]
> **100% Private to Your Device:** Your API key is saved strictly inside your phone's local storage (`localStorage`). It is never sent to or stored on any external server. Make sure to paste your key **after** opening the app from your phone's Home Screen so it stays saved permanently.

##  Step 3: How to Use the FreightShorts Terminal

### **1. Navigating the Feed**
* **Swipe Up / Down:** Swipe vertically anywhere on a card (or tap **`↑ Prev`** / **`Next ↓`** in the bottom dock) to flip through 60-word executive briefs.
* **Filter by Pillar:** Tap any of the category chips below the top bar (**Fraud & Safety**, **Cost & Capacity**, **Regulations**, **AI & Tech**, or **🎓 Academy**) to filter the deck on the fly.
* **Refresh Live Feeds (`↻`):** Tap the **`↻`** button in the top-right corner at any time to pull the latest published industry articles.

### **2. Adjusting Domain Academy Complexity (`🎓 Tier 201 / 301 / 401`)**
* Every 4th card in your feed is a gold-bordered **Domain Academy** masterclass.
* Tap the **`🎓 Tier`** dropdown in the top header to choose your technical depth:
  * **Tier 201:** Fleet unit economics, Operating Ratio (OR) elasticity, and double-brokering mechanics.
  * **Tier 301:** Quantitative routing-guide waterfall decay, OTRI convexity, and federal preemption tort risk.
  * **Tier 401:** C-Suite network topology, Inbound Cross-Dock (IXD) queueing fragility, and autonomous corridor bifurcation.

### **3. Using Level 2 Deep Dive & Ask Gemini AI**
* **Open the Executive Brief:** Tap the blue **`✨ Level 2 & Ask AI`** button in the bottom dock (or the teaser bar inside the card) to slide up the full analysis sheet:
  * **Top KPI Strip:** 3 key figures and metrics extracted from the story.
  * **∑ Executive Model:** The core financial or operational equation governing the topic.
  * **3-Part Breakdown:** Color-coded cards for *1. Underlying Mechanics & Math*, *2. Second-Order Network Effects*, and *3. Actionable Executive Playbook*.
* **Ask Follow-Up Questions (`💬 Ask Gemini AI` Tab):**
  * At the top of the slide-up sheet, tap **`💬 Ask Gemini AI`**.
  * Tap any of the **3 suggested executive prompt chips** for an instant analysis, or type your own question in the bottom chat bar to stress-test how the news impacts your network.
* **Live AI Card Rewrite (`✨ AI` Button in Top Header):**
  * Viewing a raw news story and want a sharper quantitative breakdown? Tap **`✨ AI`** in the top header to have Gemini dynamically rewrite the card with a fresh 60-word thesis, formula, and custom KPI pills.

### **4. Listening Hands-Free (`🎧 Audio` Podcast Mode)**
* Tap **`🎧 Audio`** in the bottom thumb dock (`🎙️ ON`).
* The app will read the source, headline, 60-word summary, and Level 2 breakdown aloud—and **automatically swipe to the next card** and keep reading until you tap **`🎙️ ON`** again to pause.
